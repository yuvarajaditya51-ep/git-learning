Early Life

Dhoni grew up in Ranchi and was first a football goalkeeper at school. His football coach noticed his reflexes and sent him to play cricket as a wicketkeeper.

He played domestic cricket for Bihar and then Jharkhand. Before his cricket career took off, he worked as a ticket collector at Kharagpur railway station. His performances for India A in 2004 got him selected for the national team.

International Career

Timeline

  1. 2004: ODI debut against Bangladesh (23 December).
  2. 2005: Test debut against Sri Lanka in Chennai (2 December). Earlier that year he scored 148 against Pakistan and 183* against Sri Lanka in ODIs.
  3. 2006: T20I debut against South Africa (1 December).
  4. 2007: Became India captain and led a young team to the T20 World Cup title.
  5. 2008: Became Test captain. India reached the No. 1 Test ranking in 2009.
  6. 2011: Led India to the ODI World Cup, finishing the final with a six at the Wankhede Stadium.
  7. 2013: Led India to the ICC Champions Trophy.
  8. 2014: Retired from Test cricket.
  9. 2017: Stepped down as captain in ODIs and T20Is.
  10. 2020: Retired from international cricket (15 August).

International Statistics

  • Tests: 90 matches, 4,876 runs
  • ODIs: 350 matches, 10,773 runs
  • T20Is: 98 matches, 1,617 runs

Dhoni is the only captain to win all three major ICC white-ball trophies: the T20 World Cup, the ODI World Cup and the Champions Trophy.

Franchise Career (IPL)

Dhoni joined Chennai Super Kings at the first IPL auction in 2008 and captained the team for most of his career.

Highlights

  • IPL titles with CSK:
    • 2010
    • 2011
    • 2018
    • 2021
    • 2023
  • Won the Champions League T20 with CSK in 2010 and 2014.
  • Played for Rising Pune Supergiant in 2016 and 2017, when CSK was suspended.
  • Returned to CSK in 2018 and won the title in the same season.
  • Finished with 5,439 runs in 278 IPL matches.

Latest update

On 17 November 2026, Dhoni retired from the IPL as a player. He will continue with Chennai Super Kings as team mentor from IPL 2027.

Awards and Legacy

  • Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna (2007-08)
  • Padma Shri (2009)
  • Padma Bhushan (2018)
  • Honorary Lieutenant Colonel in the Territorial Army (2011)

Fans remember Dhoni for his calm leadership, quick stumpings and his skill at finishing matches under pressure.
