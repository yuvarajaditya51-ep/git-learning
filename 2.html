Mahendra Singh Dhoni

Former Captain of the Indian National Cricket Team & Chennai Super Kings

Career Life

Born on July 7, 1981, in Ranchi, Jharkhand, Mahendra Singh Dhoni transitioned from sports, famously working as a Ticket Examiner with Indian Railways before his breakthrough. Known as "Captain Cool" and "Thala", he holds an honorary Lieutenant Colonel rank in the Territorial Army.

International Career

  • Debut & Rise: Debuted in ODIs (2004) and Tests (2005), renowned for his finishing prowess and wicketkeeping.
  • ICC Trophies: The only captain to win all three major ICC limited-overs trophies: the 2007 ICC World Twenty20, 2011 ICC Cricket World Cup, and 2013 ICC Champions Trophy.
  • Legacy: Led India to No. 1 in Test rankings before retiring from international cricket.

Franchise Career

  • CSK Captaincy: Drafted by Chennai Super Kings in 2008, guiding them to multiple IPL titles.
  • Other Stints & Transition: Played for Rising Pune Supergiant during CSK's suspension and later transitioned to a leadership and mentor role.
